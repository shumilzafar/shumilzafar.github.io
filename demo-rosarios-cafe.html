From the counter

Cooked and baked in-house through the day. Most of our cakes and pastries come with a gluten-free option, and our ingredients are locally sourced wherever we can, coffee roasted for us by D J Miles in Porlock, Somerset.

Full menus are available in the café. Takeaway coffee, food and cakes to order at the counter, plus our own ground coffee to take home, for espresso maker or cafetière.

Rosario brought Sicily with him

Run by Rosario Bavetta, Rosarios aims to bring a bit of Sicily to the West Country. Rosario has poured his passion for coffee, and his nonna's cooking, into this curiously fabulous café.

You'll find us on Northumberland Place, one of Bath's ancient cut-through alleyways between the Guildhall and the main shopping streets. Inside the Grade II listed Georgian building there's a ground-floor counter of Italian and homemade cakes, tables outside for people-watching, and cosy seating upstairs looking down on Bath's independent shops.

We source locally to support local farming, and use authentic Italian ingredients in the kitchen. Our tea blender and coffee roaster, D J Miles of Porlock, works with the Ethical Tea Partnership, and our beans come from small co-operatives.

"It's the perfect place to stop for a bite while you enjoy shopping around Bath's famous small independent shops."

Find us

Two minutes from Bath Abbey, tucked down Northumberland Place. Come in for table service, or grab a takeaway coffee on the way through.
