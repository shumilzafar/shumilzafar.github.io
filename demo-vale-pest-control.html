What we deal with

Every job starts with a proper look around. Most pest problems have a reason, a gap, a drain, a harbourage point, and we will point it out as we go, so the problem stays solved.

01

Rats, mice & drain surveys

Full inspection from top to bottom, proofing advice and, where it helps, a CCTV drain survey with proper repair guidance. Trapping offered as an alternative to poison. Ask about a survey

02

Honey bees & bumble bees

Our speciality. Live extraction from cavity walls, chimneys, roof voids and soffits, bees and stores removed, comb taken out. Bumble bee nests relocated alive wherever accessible. How bee extraction works

03

Bed bugs & fleas

Thorough harbourage inspection with steam treatment where needed, plus monitoring to confirm the job is done. ULV fogging available to flush fleas into treated areas the same evening.

04

Squirrels & wildlife

Squirrels trapped and removed within days in most cases, no rodenticide, so no risk to birds of prey. Also rabbits, fox problems, moles and badger advice.

05

Birds & proofing

Pigeon, gull and starling control with proofing against roosting and nesting. Guano cleanup advice and prevention so they do not come straight back.

06

Wasps, ants & insects

Wasp nest removal, carpet beetles, clothes moths, cluster flies, cockroaches and ants. If you are not sure what you have, send us a photo, we will identify it first.

Bees in your walls? We take them out alive.

Because we are beekeepers as well as pest controllers, we can offer something only a handful of UK firms do: live honey bee extraction. Rather than poisoning a colony in situ, which leaves dead bees, rotting larvae and rancid honey inside your wall, we remove the bees and their stores and leave only clean wax comb behind.

We extract from cavity walls, chimneys, roof voids, above soffits and under tiles. We even trained as chimney sweeps for a week so chimney work is done correctly.

“The bees are amazing, and so are we.”

  • Bumble bee nests removed and relocated alive where accessible
  • Honest advice first, if a nest can safely be left alone, we will tell you
  • Photo identification welcome before we visit

How we work

No mess, no fuss, and no unpleasant surprises. Prices are competitive, fully explained before any work commences, and compared honestly against local council charges.

STEP ONE

Inspect properly

We look at the property and surroundings, find where pests are getting in and why they are there, and talk you through it as we go.

STEP TWO

Agree the price

You get a fixed price and the options, trapping versus baiting, treatment versus monitoring, explained in plain English before we start.

STEP THREE

Treat & proof

Safe, proven methods, family-friendly where it matters, with proofing and follow-up visits built in where the label and the law require them.

Targeted, low-volume treatments, including ULV fogging at around 15 microns, so treatment reaches harbourage points without drenching your home.
