Good to know before you book

The useful bits from my service agreement, in plain English.

How does booking & payment work?

A 50% deposit is due one week before your appointment, with the balance paid when you collect your dog. Deposits are transferable if you cancel with at least 48 hours' notice, or refunded if no future booking is made.

What if I need to cancel?

Either of us can end the agreement with at least 48 hours' written notice (email is fine). With less than 48 hours' notice, the full agreed payment may be charged.

What should I bring / tell you?

Please make sure your dog has a collar and tag, and complete the registration form before your first groom, including any medical conditions or special needs that could affect the groom. Accurate info keeps your dog safe.

Extra charges, de-matting & fleas

Heavy de-matting takes extra time and is charged additionally. If fleas are found, there's an additional charge, I'll notify you straight away and your dog will need collecting as soon as possible.

Emergencies

If anything happens, I'll contact you first on the numbers from your registration form. If I can't reach you in time, I'm authorised to transport your dog to your listed vet, request on-site vet treatment, or go to an emergency clinic if needed.
