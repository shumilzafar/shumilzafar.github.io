A worn-out piece is not finished. It is just waiting its turn.

Most jobs start the same way: a seat that has split, a chair nobody wants to throw out, or a convertible top that has given up. The repair is rarely mysterious. It just takes the right material, a careful pattern and somebody who knows where the seams should land.

Start with the piece you almost replaced.

A split seam or sun-baked seat does not always mean the whole thing is done. Call the shop and ask what can be repaired, recovered or rebuilt.

Do you only work on cars?

No. The shop handles residential and commercial furniture repairs alongside automotive interiors, boats, aircraft, motorcycles and heavy duty machinery.

Can an original interior be matched?

Original automotive fabrics are one of the shop’s listed services. Bring the vehicle or a clear look at the existing interior so the material can be assessed.

Do you install leather kits and seat heaters?

Yes. Braithwaite’s is a Katzkin leather interior dealer and a preferred installer for Pacific seat heaters.

How do I get a job looked at?

Call (306) 664-2251 or email info@braithwaitesupholstery.ca and describe the vehicle or piece.
