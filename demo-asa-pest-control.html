Saskatoon pests, season by season

Our climate drives the pest calendar. This is what we get called about most, and when.

Spring

Carpenter ants wake up, cluster flies appear, and wasps start scouting nest sites.

Summer

Peak season for wasps, ants and bed bugs, especially in rentals and multi-unit buildings.

Fall

Mice start looking for a warm place to spend the winter. This is when exclusion work pays off.

Winter

Cockroaches and bed bugs stay active indoors where it's heated. Mice are already inside.
