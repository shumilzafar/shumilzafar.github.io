What got fixed on this page

Nothing about the business changed, same services, same prices, same phone number. Just the website problems that were sitting in plain sight:

1

The contact form was showing raw code

The homepage literally displayed [contact-form-7 id="1344"] as page text, so visitors had no way to request an estimate online. It's now the working form at the bottom of this page.

2

A placeholder email was live

One of the two contact blocks listed example@example.com, anyone emailing it got nowhere. Everything now points to the real address, lane@v3landscape.com.

3

Contact info appeared twice

The "Reach Out To Us" section was duplicated back-to-back with two different emails. It's merged into one clear contact block, with click-to-call and click-to-email throughout.

4

Typos and loose ends cleaned up

Small things that make a business look unfinished: "heatlhy" in the fertilization blurb, "COE" in the review names, stray dollar signs in the pricing tables. All corrected, and the pricing is laid out so it's actually readable on a phone.

5

Pricing that contradicted itself

The packages listed weekly mowing from $45 a visit, while a $79/month plan also claimed to include weekly mowing, four visits alone would run about $180. The per-visit rates are now labeled per visit, and the plan sticker prices are off the page; monthly rates get confirmed with the free estimate, so nothing on the page disagrees with itself.
